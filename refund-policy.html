Refund Policy

Last Updated: November 13, 2026

This Refund Policy explains how cancellations and refund requests work for Rostr subscriptions provided by Gamma Tech & Services LLP. Please read it alongside our Terms of Service.

Subscriptions purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play are subject to that store's refund policies. Cancelling a subscription stops future renewals; it does not automatically refund a previous payment.

1. Subscription Payments

Paid Rostr subscriptions are billed through the Apple App Store or Google Play. The store used for your purchase manages billing and refund processing. Refund eligibility, approval, and processing times depend on the applicable store policy and your local consumer protection laws.

2. Cancelling Your Subscription

You may cancel your subscription at any time through the store account used to purchase it:

  • Apple App Store: Open Settings on your Apple device, select your name, then Subscriptions, select Rostr, and choose Cancel Subscription. See Apple's cancellation instructions.
  • Google Play: Open Google Play, select your profile, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, select Rostr, and choose Cancel subscription. See Google Play's cancellation instructions.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and you can normally continue using paid features until then. Uninstalling Rostr or deleting your Rostr account does not cancel your app store subscription. Cancel it separately through the relevant store to prevent further charges.

3. Requesting a Refund

3.1 Apple App Store Purchases

  1. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple Account used for your purchase.
  2. Choose "Request a refund," select the reason, and select your Rostr purchase.
  3. Submit the request and follow Apple's instructions to check its status.

Apple reviews refund requests under its policies. Rostr cannot approve or issue refunds for Apple App Store purchases. For more information, see Apple's refund guidance.

3.2 Google Play Purchases

Follow Google Play's refund instructions using the Google Account used for your purchase. If Google Play directs you to contact the developer, email us at hi@getrostr.com with your order details so we can review the request under the applicable policies and law.

4. Unused Time and Renewals

We do not offer partial refunds for unused portions of a subscription period, except where required by applicable law or allowed by the relevant app store's refund policy. Renewal charges are subject to the same refund process as other subscription payments. A refund request does not guarantee approval.

5. Billing Problems

If you notice an unexpected or duplicate charge, or paid features are unavailable after a purchase, contact us for help. Include your Rostr account email, the store used, purchase date, order or transaction ID, and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords, full card numbers, or other sensitive payment credentials.

If a refund is approved, the store or payment provider determines when and how it is returned. Paid access may change when a refund is processed.

6. Your Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy limits any refund or other consumer rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law. Where mandatory law or the applicable app store policy provides additional rights, those rights apply.
